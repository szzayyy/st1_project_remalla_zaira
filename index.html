## About Me I am Zaira D. Remalla, an Information Technology student who is interested in learning more about technology and developing my skills outside the classroom. As a student, I believe that learning does not only happen through regular school subjects. I also learn by attending online seminars, webinars, training sessions, and courses related to Information Technology. I created this project to document some of the online learning activities I have attended. Through these seminars and courses, I was able to explore different topics such as Information Technology fundamentals, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, database management, networking, computer hardware, digital content, and Internet of Things. These experiences helped me gain additional knowledge and encouraged me to continue improving my technical skills. The certificates, badges, and attendance proofs included in this website represent my participation and learning experiences as an IT student.

About this website

About the Seminars and Tours Website Seminars and Tours - is a personal website created as my midterm project to showcase and organize the online seminars, webinars, courses, and learning activities I have attended. The website serves as a digital collection of my certificates, digital badges, and proof of attendance. It organizes the records into three categories: **Certificates, Badges, and Proof of Attendance**, making it easier to browse and find a specific learning activity. The website includes learning activities from different providers and organizations, including **UpSkillTech, Cisco, IBM SkillBuild, and BlackStride Cyber**. Some of the topics included are Information Technology Fundamentals, AI, cybersecurity, database management, computer hardware, networking, Internet of Things, and digital content. Each entry can also be verified through a link connected to its corresponding Google Drive file. This allows the certificates and other documents to be viewed as supporting evidence of my participation. The main purpose of this website is to keep my learning records organized in one place while also presenting my continuous learning and development as an Information Technology student.
